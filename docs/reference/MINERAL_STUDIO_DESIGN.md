# Design System: drishvo.co.in - Mineral Studio
Status: Selected exploration direction; homepage concepts await review.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
A modern, minimal creative video studio. Bright, composed and precise, with editorial asymmetry and purposeful motion. Professional yet imaginative. Density 4/10, variance 6/10, motion 4/10. Product: English 60-90-second educational motion-graphics videos. Four-step workflow: Idea, Storyboard, Video, Publish. Internal release; no public signup or billing.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- Mineral Canvas #F4F6F5: main background.
- Clean Surface #FFFFFF: raised content and input surfaces.
- Charcoal Ink #202725: main text.
- Quiet Sage #596560: secondary text.
- Structural Mist #D8E0DC: decorative separators, not sole control boundaries.
- Studio Teal #16766A: single accent, primary buttons and selected states.
- Deep Teal #115E55: hover.
- White #FFFFFF: on primary buttons; measured contrast approximately 5.48:1.
- Pale Mineral #E4F0EC: subtle selected surfaces.
Neutral surfaces dominate; saturated accent covers at most roughly 5% of the page. Functional error/warning colors are exceptions and must be labeled. No purple, neon, gradient text or pure black.

## 3. Typography Rules
Geist for display and interface, weights 400, 500 and 600. Headlines 36-64px responsive, line-height 1.08, tracking -0.025em. Section headings 24-32px. Body 16px, line-height 1.6, maximum 65ch. Labels 14px; secondary metadata 12px. Tabular figures for duration and scene numbering. No Inter, serif dashboard fonts or oversized shouting headlines.
Wordmark is lowercase drishvo.co.in, with a quieter but legible domain suffix.

## 4. Component Stylings
Primary buttons: solid teal, white text, 10px corners, 44px minimum touch height, tactile press feedback, no glow. One primary action per section; homepage primary CTA is Open studio for internal users. Inputs: label above, inline help/error below, 10px corners, visible focus. Preview surfaces: 16px corners, restrained borders and shadows only when needed. Cards only represent actual projects or scenes. Honest loading stages; no fake metrics, logos, testimonials or completed integrations.

## 5. Layout Principles
Max width 1200px, disciplined grid, spacing 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96px. Left-aligned asymmetric hero. Small contextual diagram or scene detail may punctuate headline typography without overlap. Use real product motifs: narration blocks, schematic frames, editable storyboard and vertical 9:16 video. Clear spatial separation; no floating stack overlaps. No three equal feature cards.
Below 768px all multi-column areas become one column; 20px side padding, no horizontal overflow. Keep labels readable and touch areas >=44px. Homepage is a visual concept; preserve the internal-only release and product boundaries.

## 6. Motion & Interaction
120-180ms control feedback; 220-320ms scene transitions. Transform/opacity only. Springs if needed: stiffness 100, damping 20. Subtle looping cue only for an active progress state or explicitly playable demo; idle UI stays still per the user's restrained professional brief. Respect reduced motion, provide pause for long demos. No scroll hijacking or perpetual decorative movement.

## 7. Anti-Patterns
No neon, robots, sparkle motifs, generic chrome spheres, unrelated stock photos, custom cursor, gradient headlines, emoji, fake social proof, pricing tables or subscription UI. Avoid centered slogan plus three identical cards. No claims that live voices, Instagram publishing, latency or production readiness have been verified. Use clear copy such as Create storyboard, Review video and Approve & generate. No AI marketing cliches.
