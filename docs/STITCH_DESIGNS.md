## Requested glass alternative
The user liked the initial design and requested a glassy UI exploration. Mineral Glass — Frosted Studio retains Geist, charcoal text and the teal accent. Use translucent white surfaces (78–88% opacity), 16–24px backdrop blur, fine edges and gentle shadows. Glass is concentrated around navigation and product-preview framing; reading surfaces remain mostly opaque. Soft teal background illumination makes the effect visible. Maintain contrast and provide an opaque fallback; no neon or overlapping card piles. This is a separate exploratory homepage, not a replacement for the selected base design system.
